# Product overview

Demo shop helps small makers sell online: a storefront, checkout with
discount codes, and two plans (monthly and annual).

## Who uses it

- **Makers** run a shop of 5–50 products.
- **Their customers** buy, often from a phone, often with a code from an email.

## What matters now

1. Checkout must never lose a sale over a typo.
2. Signups: the trial starts without a card.
